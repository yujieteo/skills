# Sources and evidence

Use sources according to the population they measure. A source's presence does
not make two datasets comparable.

## Fundamentals

- [VGC Guide](https://www.vgcguide.com/) supplies the working vocabulary for
  teambuilding and battling fundamentals. Use it to interpret data, not to infer
  the current metagame.
- [In the Cells of the Eggplant](https://metarationality.com/) supplies the
  meta-rational method. Formal categories and measures depend on context,
  purpose, and reasonable judgment. Use ontological remodeling when the current
  categories hide distinctions that matter for play.

## Current data

- [Pikalytics](https://pikalytics.com/) measures usage and related ladder data.
  Record the game, format, regulation or ruleset, rating population, sample size,
  and update date exposed by the page. Treat ladder usage as ladder usage.
- [LabMaus](https://labmaus.net/home) provides tournament and team data. Record
  the event, date, phase, attendance or team count, data completeness, and the
  exact statistic used.
- [VGC Top Cut Explorer](https://cut-explorer.stalruth.dev/) provides top-cut
  event views. Record the year, regulation, event, cut size, and whether the view
  represents teams, Pokemon slots, leads, wins, or another unit.

## Source hierarchy

Use official rules and event records for regulation dates and event facts. Use
the listed data tools for observations they expose. Link the exact page or query,
not a home page, when a stable result URL exists.

If a site is unavailable, report the missing coverage. Continue only when the
remaining evidence supports a narrower claim. Do not replace a missing primary
dataset with unsourced social posts, tier lists, or memory.

## Comparison checks

Before comparing two values, confirm all of these dimensions or explain the
difference:

```toon
comparison_dimensions[7]:
  regulation
  time_window
  population
  observational_unit
  event_phase
  conditioning
  source_freshness
```

For top-cut conversion, report entrants or day-two teams when the source exposes
them. A top-cut share alone is not a conversion rate. For a Pokemon, core, or
archetype, declare whether membership can overlap. For trends across events,
show event-level values before an aggregate so one large event does not silently
dominate the conclusion.
